## What an SLCD is

An **SLCD** (spring-loaded camming device) is a mechanical chock with 3 or 4 lobes mounted on an axle. Pulling a trigger retracts the lobes, allowing the unit to slip into a crack; releasing the trigger lets the lobes spring outward to grip the crack walls. Designed by Ray Jardine and commercialized by Wild Country as **Friends** in 1978 (source: how-to-rock-climb.pdf, pp. 117, 131).

## Flexible vs. rigid stem

- **Flexible stem** — wire cable. Bends without leveraging the placement out of the rock in a horizontal crack. Modern standard.
- **Rigid stem** — solid bar. Strong but in a horizontal crack the lever arm of the bar can break the unit on a fall. Older designs; largely retired.

(source: how-to-rock-climb.pdf, pp. 133–135)

## Placement essentials

A good cam placement (source: how-to-rock-climb.pdf, pp. 138, 140):

1. **Crack walls are solid** — no flakes, no detached blocks.
2. **All lobes contact the rock** — the unit isn't tipped sideways.
3. **Lobes engaged 50–90%** — not fully open (won't grip on shock-load), not fully closed (won't expand if the crack widens slightly).
4. **Stem oriented in the [[direction-of-pull]]**.
5. **Extended with a [[carabiners|quickdraw]]** if the route wanders, so rope drag doesn't walk the cam.

## Dangerous placements

- **Too tight** (lobes nearly closed) — won't expand to grip if the crack widens.
- **Too loose / tipped out** — only some lobes contact rock; pull-out test fails.
- **Offset** (one side bigger than the other) — lobes load unevenly.
- **In flared cracks** without a designated offset cam — lobes can walk out.
- **Behind expanding flakes** — flake levers outward, ejecting cam.

(source: how-to-rock-climb.pdf, pp. 136–137)

## Getting unstuck

A cam that has walked deep into a crack or had its trigger crammed can be a recovery project. Use a nut tool or a "cam-jack" wire to pull the trigger from behind. Sometimes you leave them (source: how-to-rock-climb.pdf, p. 139).

## Maintenance

- Rinse with water after sandy / muddy / salty climbing.
- Oil the axle and trigger lightly.
- Inspect the sling — replace if frayed, faded, or older than ~5 years.
- Retire a cam with bent lobes, frozen trigger, or cracked stem.

(source: how-to-rock-climb.pdf, p. 139)

## Racking

Cams rack one-per-biner on the harness gear loop, sorted by size, gates facing the body (source: how-to-rock-climb.pdf, p. 219).
